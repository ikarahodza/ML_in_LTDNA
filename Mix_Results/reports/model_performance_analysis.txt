5. OVERALL CONCLUSIONS:

   - Resampling strategies significantly improve performance for models struggling with class imbalance
   - TabPFN is unique - it performs best without resampling, suggesting it handles imbalance internally
   - For production use, LGBM with RandomOverSampler or XGBoost with SMOTE recommended
